A requirement is one legal point the answer must settle for the reader, stated in the question's own terms: whether the Act applies to the situation at all, what a term means, on what basis an act is allowed, what a party must do, what a data subject may demand, what the consequence of a breach is, or what steps and time limits apply. Set issue_type to the kind of point it is. Do not add a requirement the question does not raise, and do not split one point into several. When the question asks what must be done or whether something is allowed, whether the Act or the duty applies to that situation is itself a point to settle (issue_type scope_or_exemption) only if the question's own facts raise it (who is acting, for what purpose, where); do not add it mechanically.

Do not name a section number, a condition, an amount or a conclusion in a requirement: the compiler has not read the statute, and the investigator would read such text as fact. Describe what must be found, not what the answer is. answer_requirements carry only presentation wording the question itself states (for example: answer yes or no first, list the steps, state the maximum penalty).
